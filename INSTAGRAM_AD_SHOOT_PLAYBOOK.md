# Instagram scroll-stopping ad shoot playbook
### Triplooper B2B ad campaign assets

This playbook contains four ready-to-shoot video concepts to stop the scroll of boutique hotel, resort, and restaurant owners on Instagram. The scripts are written in a direct, natural voice, focusing on daily operational headaches and clear solutions.

---

## Visual and tone guidelines

- **Style:** Realistic and slightly cinematic. Avoid looking like a glossy corporate commercial. It should look like a video shot by a real hotel owner or a guest.
- **Pacing:** Use fast-cut hooks in the first three seconds, followed by a steady, clear demonstration of the software.
- **Hardware:** Shoot entirely on a modern smartphone with a basic clip-on microphone for clean audio.
- **Tone:** Practical, direct, and slightly frustrated with the status quo, transitioning to calm and organized.

## Smartphone directing and shooting guide
*How to get premium results on a budget*

### Camera settings
Record vertically using the 9:16 aspect ratio. Set your phone video to 4K at 30fps or 1080p at 60fps for clean, smooth movements. If your phone has a 2x zoom lens, use it for close-up shots like counting cash or tapping screens. This prevents wide-angle distortion and keeps the focus tight. Keep the camera steady by using a basic tripod or resting your phone on a solid surface. Shaky footage immediately makes the ad look cheap.

### Lighting and background
Shoot near large windows during daylight. Avoid harsh overhead lights that cast deep shadows. When filming phone or tablet screens, turn down the device brightness slightly so the camera doesn't overexpose it. Keep backgrounds tidy by clearing desks, removing stray bottles, wires, or messy papers.

### Audio recording
Do not record your voiceover in a large, empty room where it will echo. Record in a small room with soft furnishings like curtains or a carpet to absorb bounce. Use a basic lapel microphone plugged into your phone, or hold your phone close to your mouth when recording the voice track. Clear audio is crucial for stopping scrolls.

### Editing and text overlays
Place your main hook text in bold, high-contrast letters in the center of the screen immediately. Do not build up to it. Cut out every pause, breath, and silence in editing. The visual should change every two to three seconds to keep viewers engaged. Use low-volume, rhythmic background beats. Choose acoustic guitar or ambient synth beats instead of generic corporate tunes. Use clean, bold sans-serif fonts in white with a subtle drop shadow for readability.
