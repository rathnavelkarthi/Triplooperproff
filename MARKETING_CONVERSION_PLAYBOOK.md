# Triplooper (EasyStay): Marketing & Sales Conversion Playbook

This guide is designed to prepare the marketing and sales outreach teams to pitch Triplooper (formerly EasyStay) to hotel owners, managers, and restaurant operators. It provides core value propositions, step-by-step conversion strategies, objection handling, and outreach scripts.

---

## 🌟 1. Core Value Proposition: The "Elevator Pitch"

When pitching to a property owner, do not just sell "software." Sell **automation, guest satisfaction, and extra revenue**.

> *"Triplooper is an all-in-one Property Management System (PMS), Restaurant POS, and Guest Portal built specifically for modern boutique hotels and resorts. Instead of paying for three separate systems, you get a single platform that handles booking check-ins, restaurant orders, room service, and driver partnerships. The best part? Your guests don't need to download any apps — they simply scan a QR code in their room to order food, call for service, or request local tours directly from their phones."*

### Key Selling Points:
* **All-in-One System:** Hotel PMS + Restaurant billing + Room Service + Local Guide.
* **Frictionless Guest Experience:** QR-code based web portal. No downloads, no passwords.
* **Instant Digital Menu:** Guests order food directly to their rooms, automatically billing to their tab.
* **Local Monetization:** Partner with local drivers and guide services directly through the platform.

## 📈 2. Step-by-Step Lead Conversion Funnel

The goal is to move a property owner from a cold lead to a verified, billing partner.

```
  [1] The Pitch  ──>  [2] Quick Registration  ──>  [3] Verification  ──>  [4] Active Billing
  (Solve a Pain)        (Setup Subdomain)            (Upload Docs)         (Complete Setup)
```

### Step 1: The Initial Hook
* **Action:** Highlight their current inefficiency. Usually, they are using paper logbooks or outdated, expensive desktop software.
* **Script:** *"How do your guests currently order food or request housekeeping? What if they could do it in one tap on their phone, and it went straight to your kitchen screen?"*

### Step 2: Frictionless Sign Up
* **Action:** Direct them to register their property. It takes under 60 seconds.
* **Outcome:** They get their own dedicated subdomain (e.g. `arfunest.triplooper.com`) to test the interface immediately.

### Step 3: The Verification Push (CRITICAL)
* **Action:** Explain that to go live and begin taking real guest bookings and transactions, they must upload verification documents for security and trust.
* **Why:** This builds platform integrity and prevents unauthorized listings.
* **Reminder:** Guide them to the `/admin/verification` screen to upload their **Government Trade License** and **Complaints Proof**.

## ❓ 3. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) & Objection Handling

Here are the most common questions hotel owners will ask your sales team:

### Q1: "How much does it cost?"
* **Answer:** *"We have flexible tiers. Our **Starter Plan** is perfect for boutique properties looking to get verified and set up. We also offer **Growth** and **Enterprise** plans as your room count and feature requirements scale. For reservations and bookings handled through the platform, we apply a standard, transparent 12% commission, which means we only make money when you do."*

### Q2: "Do my guests have to download an app from the App Store?"
* **Answer:** *"No! App downloads kill guest usage. With Triplooper, guests scan a unique QR code in their room. It immediately opens a secure mobile-optimized web portal in their browser. They can order food, chat with reception, and request towels instantly."*

### Q3: "What if I run both a hotel and a restaurant?"
* **Answer:** *"Triplooper is built for this. During registration, you can choose your business type: **Hotel**, **Restaurant**, or **Combined**. The system merges room service and table ordering, allowing restaurant bills to be posted directly to the guest’s final room invoice."*

### Q4: "Is our data secure? Why do we need to upload government documents?"
* **Answer:** *"Security is our top priority. We verify government trade licenses to ensure only legitimate, licensed properties are listed on Triplooper. This protects your brand, prevents fraudulent bookings, and secures guest payments. All documents are stored in secure, encrypted private servers."*

### Q5: "How does the Driver and Local Guide feature work?"
* **Answer:** *"You can register local drivers (cabs, tour guides) under your portal. When guests want to book a tour or a trip to an attraction, they can see the driver’s profile and contact details directly in their guest portal, helping you provide local experiences and monetize partnerships."*
