# EasyStay — All-in-One Hotel & Restaurant Management Platform

---

## PAGE 1 — What The App Does Today

### One Dashboard, Two Businesses
Run a **hotel**, a **restaurant**, or **both together** from a single login. Role-based access for owners, managers, reception, housekeeping, servers, and accountants — everyone sees only what they need.

### Hotel Management
- **Reservations** — create, edit, track every booking in one place
- **Calendar View** — visual room availability at a glance
- **Rooms** — live room status: clean, occupied, out-of-service
- **Channel Manager** — sync rooms & rates across booking sites, no double-bookings
- **Service Requests** — guest requests routed straight to housekeeping/reception
- **Pricing Rules** — seasonal, occupancy-based, and dynamic rate control
- **Booking Settings** — check-in/out rules, deposits, policies

### Restaurant Management
- **Point of Sale (POS)** — fast billing at the counter
- **Server Orders** — waiters take orders table-side from their phone
- **Menu Manager** — build menu, categories, prices, photos
- **Tables** — live table status, seating, and turnover
- **Food Orders** — kitchen order tracking end to end
- **Inventory** — stock tracking with low-stock awareness

### Guest Experience
- **Own Booking Website** — every property gets a branded site, editable in-app
- **Guest Chat** — direct guest-to-property messaging
- **Digital Guest Bill** — guests view their bill on their phone
- **WhatsApp Integration** — booking confirmations, credentials, and alerts over WhatsApp
- **Trip Planner** — budget-based trip & stay recommendation wizard

### Money & Insight
- **Reports** — occupancy, revenue, and sales analytics
- **Profit & Loss** — real numbers, real margins
- **Expenses & Savings Margin** — track cost vs. earning
- **Invoices & Payments** — professional invoices, PDF export, print
- **QR Print** — table/room QR codes for menus and self-service

### Built-In Essentials
- Multi-property support — manage a whole group from one account
- Staff Management — add staff, assign roles, auto-send login credentials
- Website Editor & CMS — update rooms, content, and landing page without a developer
